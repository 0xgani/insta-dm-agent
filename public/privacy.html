Privacy Policy

Exovise Instagram Assistant ("Auto DM Replier") · Effective November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information our automated Instagram direct-message assistant collects when you message our Instagram account, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your messages to us and our replies, including messages written by our team.
  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID and public username, provided by Instagram when you message us.
  • The type of non-text messages you send (for example "image" or "voice message"). We do not download or store the media itself.

We do not collect your password, contacts, location, or any information from outside your conversation with our account.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions automatically, using an AI model that sees your recent messages and our business information.
  • To pass your conversation to a member of our team when you ask to talk to a person.
  • To keep context so a later reply understands what you asked before.

We do not use your messages for advertising, we do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Who we share it with

  • Anthropic (the Claude AI model) processes your recent messages to write a reply. Anthropic acts as our service provider and does not use this data to train its models.
  • Telegram: when you ask for a person, our team gets a notification with your Instagram username.
  • Meta / Instagram, which delivers the messages, under Instagram's own privacy policy.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep only the 20 most recent messages of each conversation. Older messages are deleted automatically. Technical records used to prevent duplicate processing (message IDs, without content) are deleted after 14 days.

Security

Data is stored on a server we control. Access to it and to the assistant's settings is protected by authentication.

Your choices and data deletion

  • You can ask for a real person at any time by replying "I want to talk to a person".
  • To delete your data, send a direct message to @gani.meta.test saying "delete my data". We will delete your stored conversation and username within 30 days and confirm in the chat.
  • You can also stop at any time by not messaging us. Blocking our account stops all processing.

Children

This service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by Instagram in your country).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @gani.meta.test on Instagram.
